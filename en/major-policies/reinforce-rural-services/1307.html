Enhance Digital Services in Remote Areas

  • Share
    • Facebook
    • Twitter
    • LINE
  • Print

Connecting Communities Through Spectrum

Telecommunications are essential to modern society. To expand mobile coverage in remote and underserved areas and promote digital applications, the Ministry of Digital Affairs (MODA) combines universal service programs, government incentive schemes, and spectrum usage fee incentives to encourage Mobile Network Operator(MNO) to invest in network infrastructure, improve connectivity, and enable innovative digital services.

Public-Private Partnership for Better Connectivityand Digital Development

Radio spectrum is a scarce public resource. Spectrum usage fees help ensure its efficient and fair use. In addition, MODA uses incentive mechanisms to encourage mobile operators to expand telecommunications services in remote and underserved areas.

  • Mountain Lodges
  • National Parks
  • Nature Trails
  • Railway Stations

MODA uses spectrum usage fee incentives to encourage MNO to make better use of spectrum resources and mobile broadband networks by partnering with government agencies and industry to develop innovative digital services.

  • Industry Verticals
  • Trust & Identity
  • Data Innovation
  • Green Sustainability
